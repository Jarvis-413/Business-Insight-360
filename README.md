Business Insights 360 – Power BI Project | Turning Data into Decisions
After working with data for years, I realized one common problem across organizations — data exists everywhere, but insights are nowhere.
So, I built Business Insights 360, an end-to-end Power BI solution designed to solve real-world business challenges across Finance, Sales, Marketing, and Supply Chain.
----------------------------------------
🔍 💡 Problem Statement
Organizations often struggle with:
❌ Data scattered across multiple systems (Excel, ERP, CRM)
 ❌ Time-consuming manual reporting
 ❌ No real-time visibility into key KPIs
 ❌ Difficulty identifying profitable vs loss-making segments
 ❌ Poor demand forecasting leading to stock issues

➡️ Result: Slow decision-making & missed business opportunities

📊 Solution – Business Insights 360 Dashboard
I designed an interactive, multi-functional Power BI dashboard that provides a 360° view of business performance.
🔹 Finance View
Full P&L Statement
Net Sales, Gross Margin, Net Profit tracking
Identifies cost drivers impacting profitability
🔹 Sales View
Customer & Product performance analysis
Profitability & Growth Matrix
Highlights high-revenue but low-margin customers
🔹 Marketing View
Revenue by channel & region
Market share trends
Performance benchmarking
🔹 Supply Chain View
Forecast Accuracy tracking (90%+)
Net Error & Risk analysis (OOS / Excess Inventory)
Helps optimize inventory planning
🔹 Executive View
High-level KPI dashboard
Enables quick, strategic decision-making

📈 🔑 Key Insights from the Dashboard
✔ Strong revenue growth but declining profitability
 ✔ High operational costs impacting margins
 ✔ Certain customers generating revenue but low profit contribution
 ✔ Forecast inaccuracies leading to inventory risks

🎯 💼 Business Outcomes / Impact
✅ Centralized data into a single source of truth
 ✅ Reduced reporting time by 70–80%
 ✅ Improved visibility into profitability drivers
 ✅ Enabled faster, data-driven decisions
 ✅ Better demand forecasting & inventory optimization

🛠 🔧 Tools & Skills Used
Power BI (Dashboarding & DAX)
Power Query (ETL)
Data Modeling (Star Schema)
Excel (Data Preparation)

💬 Final Thought
This project reflects how data is not just about numbers —
 it’s about telling the right story to drive business decisions.
